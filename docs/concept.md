# Aishka and the Blog

Tagline: A blind-first audio drama about building with an AI companion.

Format: Short English audio drama with two main voices and stylized background sound design.

Target duration: 3.5 to 4.5 minutes.

Roles:
- Denis — male human protagonist, reflective and persistent.
- Aishka — female AI companion, clear, warm, and helpful.

Creative intent:
- Keep the emotional arc of the original Russian story.
- Replace visual UI moments with audible status messages, typing, notifications, room tone, and outdoor ambience.
- Make the piece understandable without any visual context.

Structure:
1. Late-night drafting with Aishka.
2. Morning disappointment and manual editing.
3. Denis realizes the AI needs guidance.
4. A learning montage with voice notes from walks.
5. A new problem: low engagement despite good posts.
6. Ads, analysis, and community insight.
7. Joint progress and payoff.

Voice casting:
- Denis: en-US-AndrewNeural
- Aishka: en-US-AvaNeural

Sound palette:
- room_night, room_morning
- typing, notification
- walk_outside
- thinking_ai, ad_research
- success_glow

Production note:
The mix should prioritize intelligibility. Sound cues must support the story, not overpower the voices.
